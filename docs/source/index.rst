
WTMod
=====

WTMod is a compiled process-unit library for water treatment,
desalination, and water reuse modelling.

It provides fast models for:

* UF and MF pretreatment
* NF and RO pressure-driven membranes
* MD thermal membrane desalination
* pump and energy recovery units
* RO two-pass/two-stage layouts
* MBR and DMBR zero-order treatment units
